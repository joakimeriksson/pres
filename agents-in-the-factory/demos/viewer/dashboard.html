Every number here comes from a UNS topic the office dashboards subscribe to. The KPIs are computed by the MES from UNS data and published back under line/KPI. Click a topic to see it on the line and in the information flow. Quality is not measured on the sawline (grading happens after drying), so OEE = availability × performance. With comm faults, this page shows what the dashboards received: stale where it was told, silently wrong where it was not. The line viewer shows the truth.
